STPV 2026: Practical information

Venue

SPTV will take place at the University of Strathclyde campus in central Glasgow. Talks will take place in room TBD

Schedule

A schedule can be found here. Participants should arrive at around noon when we start the event with lunch.

Travel

The University of Strathclyde is located short walks away from both Glasgow Queen Street train station (five minutes) and Glasgow Central train station (10 minutes). See National Rail for train times and connections. PhD students might save money by getting a 16–25 railcard.

Internet access

Eduroam Internet access is available at the conference venue; if not already configured, you can download a configuration tool for your device here. If you do not have access to Eduroam, you can also use the 'Wifi Guest' network after creating an account.
