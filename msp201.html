MSP201 Study Group

MSP201 is a series of study groups, aimed at PhD students and other academics interested in MSP topics. We typically choose either a book to read over the course of a semester, or a paper to read over one or two sessions. The study group usually happens in person, but we might occasionally set up a Zoom link upon request.

How to join? The study group is coordinated on SPLS Zulipchat, in the #UoSstream. Everyone is welcome to join and suggest new topics!

MSP201 is usually Monday 3pm in room LT1311a in Livingstone Tower.
